How we work.

Small team, real ownership

You will own a surface end to end, not a ticket queue. Every role here ships to production and recieves equity in the company. We believe in the power of owning what you build.

Built in the gym

We test on real machines with real members. The floor tells us what to build next, not a roadmap document.

Fast, on purpose

We believe improvement comes from short cycles and a sense of urgency. Our ideology is iterate fast, fail fast, and learn fast. We would rather ship something rough and iterate than perfect it. If that's you, we'd like to meet you.
